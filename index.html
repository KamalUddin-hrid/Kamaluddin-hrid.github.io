About Me

I'm a Lecturer in Physics at the University of Dhaka, working in experimental materials physics. My research focuses on the nonlinear optical properties of doped and defect-engineered nanocomposites, which I study using Z-scan and thermal-lens techniques at the Centre for Advanced Research in Science (CARS). I'm interested in how dopants and defects change the way materials respond to intense light, and I'd like to extend this work to second-harmonic generation, ultrafast spectroscopy, and 2D materials.

Research Interests

  • Nonlinear optics of doped and defect-engineered materials
  • Symmetry breaking and second-harmonic generation (SHG) spectroscopy
  • Ultrafast light–matter interactions
  • Rare-earth doping and defect states in functional nanomaterials
  • 2D materials and their optical properties
